At the midpoint, both waves travel the same distance. With zero phase difference they reinforce each other. Set the phase to 180°: a crest from one source meets a trough from the other.

The white dots are the sources. Teal and blue show signed displacement, not brightness or energy. This is an idealized sum of two circular sine waves in relative units, without attenuation or boundaries.
