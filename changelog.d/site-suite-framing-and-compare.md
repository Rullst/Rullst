### Changed
- The website (rullst.win, English and Brazilian Portuguese) frames Rullst as an open-source Rust framework suite on its first screen, shows the live applications made with Rullst right after it, explains in plain words what the Performance numbers mean for a real application (including the throughput cost of the security layers that are on by default, where Rullst is behind Axum and what 13.1 targets), links the integration validation issue #801 and the Discord community from Integrations and the README, and links the comparison page from the landing.
